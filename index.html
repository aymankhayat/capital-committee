    01 · Orchestration
    2 stages, not 8 in a row

    Seven departments at once

    Each department is its own serverless call fired from the browser, so verdicts land as they finish. The Brain call waits for all seven to settle, which makes a committee two waits instead of eight.

    02 · Accessibility
    ΔE 3.3 → 7.4 colour-blind separation

    A ring you can read with CVD

    Blue Strategy and violet Marketing sat side by side at ΔE 3.3 under deuteranopia. Reordering the ring so similar hues never touch lifted the worst neighbouring pair to 7.4, and every hub carries its name as a backup.

    03 · 3D scene
    ~20 px hub width at full zoom-out

    Clicks that actually land

    Real clicks kept missing the tiny hubs, so each one now has an invisible pick sphere. A leftover constant had also thrown inside the render loop and silently swapped in the flat map; the fallback now logs why it fired.

    04 · Verification
    5/5 engine tests

    Numbers checked against research

    The solar preset reproduces the research sanity case: NPV +$1.71M, IRR 11.2%. Writing the tests also caught my own wrong expectation: the textbook IRR is 8.9%, not 10.65%, because NPV at 10% is already negative.
